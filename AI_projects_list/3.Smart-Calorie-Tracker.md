## Project 3: Smart Calorie Tracker

### Project Abstraction

The **Smart Calorie Tracker** is a mobile-web application designed to simplify diet logging. Instead of manually searching for and typing "one medium apple" or "two slices of pizza," users simply snap a photo of their plate.

The AI model performs **Object Detection** to identify individual food items (e.g., "Egg," "Toast," "Avocado") and then maps these items to a **Nutritional Database**. The professional "startup" edge comes from the **Volume Estimation** logic—using the relative size of the plate to estimate portion sizes and provide a total caloric breakdown (Proteins, Carbs, Fats) in a clean, visual dashboard.

---

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Any modern laptop (Intel i5/Ryzen 5).
* **Camera:** A standard smartphone camera for testing.


* **Deployment Phase:**
* **Cloud Hosting:** **Hugging Face Spaces** (Free tier) or **Streamlit Cloud**.
* **No GPU Required:** By using optimized "Lite" models, this can run on standard CPUs.



---

### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

We will use a "Model-as-a-Service" or "Library-First" approach to keep it beginner-friendly.

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **Frontend & Interface:** **Streamlit** (Excellent for building camera-upload buttons in 2 lines of code).
* **AI/Vision Engine:** **YOLOv8-Nano** (You Only Look Once).
* *Why?* The "Nano" version is incredibly fast, free, and can run on a basic laptop without a GPU. It comes pre-trained on 80+ objects, including many common foods.


* **Nutrition Logic:** **Nutritionix API** or a **Local CSV Dataset**.
* *Recommendation:* Use a free CSV dataset from **Kaggle** (e.g., "Food Nutrition Dataset") to keep it completely "offline" and free of API keys.


* **Image Handling:** **PIL (Pillow)**. (For cropping and displaying the "detected" boxes around the food).
* **Deployment:** **Hugging Face Spaces**. (It allows students to host their model and the UI for free forever).
