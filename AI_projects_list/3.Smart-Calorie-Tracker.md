## Project 3: Smart Calorie Tracker

### Project Abstraction

The **Smart Calorie Tracker** is a mobile-web application designed to simplify diet logging. Instead of manually searching for and typing "one medium apple" or "two slices of pizza," users simply snap a photo of their plate.

The AI model performs **Object Detection** to identify individual food items (e.g., "Egg," "Toast," "Avocado") and then maps these items to a **Nutritional Database**. The professional "startup" edge comes from the **Volume Estimation** logic—using the relative size of the plate to estimate portion sizes and provide a total caloric breakdown (Proteins, Carbs, Fats) in a clean, visual dashboard.
