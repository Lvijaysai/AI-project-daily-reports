## Project 4: Prescription Digitizer (OCR)

### Project Abstraction

The **Prescription Digitizer** is an AI-powered utility that bridges the gap between traditional paper prescriptions and modern digital health tracking. The system uses **Optical Character Recognition (OCR)** to identify handwritten text from a photo.

Once the text is extracted, an **NLP (Natural Language Processing)** layer parses the information to identify three key components: the **Medication Name**, the **Dosage**, and the **Frequency** (e.g., "Twice a day after food"). The professional "startup" value is the **Automatic Calendar Integration**, which takes the parsed data and generates a digital schedule with push-notification reminders for the patient.

---

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Standard laptop (8GB RAM).
* **Camera:** A smartphone camera (8MP or higher) for testing with various handwriting samples.

---

### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

To keep this professional yet accessible, we will use specialized "Handwriting-friendly" libraries.

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **UI/Frontend:** **Streamlit** (Simple "Upload Image" and "Display Schedule" interface).
* **OCR Engine:** **EasyOCR** or **Tesseract OCR**.
* *Why?* **EasyOCR** is Python-based and handles "messy" handwriting much better than traditional tools. It supports 80+ languages out of the box.


* **Text Parsing (NLP):** **SpaCy** or **Regex (Regular Expressions)**.
* *Simple Approach:* Use Regex patterns to find numbers (dosage) and keywords like "mg," "tabs," or "daily."


* **Schedule Visualization:** **Pandas**. (To create a clean table/dataframe of the medication timing).
* **Deployment:** **Hugging Face Spaces**. (Best for hosting Python-heavy OCR models for free).
