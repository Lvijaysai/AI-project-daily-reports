## Project 4: Prescription Digitizer (OCR)

### Project Abstraction

The **Prescription Digitizer** is an AI-powered utility that bridges the gap between traditional paper prescriptions and modern digital health tracking. The system uses **Optical Character Recognition (OCR)** to identify handwritten text from a photo.

Once the text is extracted, an **NLP (Natural Language Processing)** layer parses the information to identify three key components: the **Medication Name**, the **Dosage**, and the **Frequency** (e.g., "Twice a day after food"). The professional "startup" value is the **Automatic Calendar Integration**, which takes the parsed data and generates a digital schedule with push-notification reminders for the patient.
