## Project 5: Micro-Lending Credit Scorer

### Project Abstraction

The **Micro-Lending Credit Scorer** is a predictive analytics tool designed for "thin-file" borrowers (unbanked individuals). Traditional banks reject these applicants due to a lack of CIBIL/FICO scores. This project builds a model that evaluates **Alternative Data**—such as the consistency of utility bill payments (electricity/water), mobile top-up patterns, and even social/behavioral metadata—to generate a "Trust Score."

Students will build a supervised learning model that classifies applicants into "Low Risk" or "High Risk." The professional value lies in the **Explainable AI (XAI)** component, which doesn't just give a score but tells the lender *why* (e.g., "Score is high due to 12 months of on-time electricity payments").
