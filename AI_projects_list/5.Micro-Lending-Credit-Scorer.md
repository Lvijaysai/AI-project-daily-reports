## Project 5: Micro-Lending Credit Scorer

### Project Abstraction

The **Micro-Lending Credit Scorer** is a predictive analytics tool designed for "thin-file" borrowers (unbanked individuals). Traditional banks reject these applicants due to a lack of CIBIL/FICO scores. This project builds a model that evaluates **Alternative Data**—such as the consistency of utility bill payments (electricity/water), mobile top-up patterns, and even social/behavioral metadata—to generate a "Trust Score."

Students will build a supervised learning model that classifies applicants into "Low Risk" or "High Risk." The professional value lies in the **Explainable AI (XAI)** component, which doesn't just give a score but tells the lender *why* (e.g., "Score is high due to 12 months of on-time electricity payments").

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Standard laptop (Intel i5 or equivalent).
* **RAM:** 8GB (Necessary for handling tabular datasets with thousands of rows).

---

### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

Since this involves "Numbers and Tables" (Tabular Data), we will use the industry-standard "Data Science Stack."

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **ML Library:** **Scikit-learn** (For basic models) and **XGBoost** (For high-accuracy "startup grade" results).
* **UI/Frontend:** **Streamlit**. (To build a professional dashboard where a loan officer can input data and see the result instantly).
* **Data Handling:** **Pandas** & **NumPy**.
* **Explainability:** **SHAP** or **LIME** (Free libraries that explain AI decisions in simple English).
* **Free Dataset Source:** **Kaggle** (Search for "Home Credit Default Risk" or "Alternative Credit Scoring" datasets).
