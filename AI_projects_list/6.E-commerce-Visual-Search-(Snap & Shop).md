## Project 6: E-commerce Visual Search ("Snap & Shop")

### Project Abstraction

The **E-commerce Visual Search** tool allows users to find products without using words. Instead of typing "blue floral summer dress," a user simply uploads an image of an outfit they saw on social media or in real life.

The AI doesn't just "label" the image; it converts the image into a mathematical vector (an "embedding") that represents the style, pattern, and color. The system then calculates the "distance" between this vector and a pre-indexed catalog of products, returning the top 5 most similar items. This is a professional-grade project that teaches students how to handle image databases and recommendation logic.

---

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Standard laptop (8GB RAM).
* **Internet:** Required to download pre-trained models (approx. 100-500MB).

---

### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

We will use a technique called "Transfer Learning" so students don't have to train a model from scratch.

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **AI Engine:** **TensorFlow/Keras (MobileNetV2)** or **PyTorch (ResNet)**.
* *Why?* We use these "Pre-trained" models to extract features. They are already experts at recognizing shapes and textures.


* **Vector Search:** **Scipy (Spatial Distance)** or **Numpy**.
* *Simple Approach:* Use "Cosine Similarity" to compare the uploaded photo to the catalog. It's just a few lines of math in Python.


* **UI/Frontend:** **Streamlit**. (It has a great `st.file_uploader` for images and `st.columns` to display the results side-by-side).
* **Dataset:** **Fashion Product Images Dataset** (Available on Kaggle for free). Students can pick a subset of 100 images to keep the project "lightweight."
