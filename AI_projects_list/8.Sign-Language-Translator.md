## Project 8: Sign Language Translator

### Project Abstraction

The **Sign Language Translator** is a real-time communication bridge for the hearing and speech impaired. Using a standard webcam, the application detects hand landmarks and identifies the specific gestures corresponding to the Sign Language alphabet or common words (e.g., "Hello," "Thank You," "Help").

The AI analyzes the coordinates of 21 points on the human hand to recognize patterns. Once a gesture is identified, the app displays the corresponding text on the screen and uses **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** to announce it, enabling a natural flow of conversation.

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Standard laptop (8GB RAM recommended for smooth video processing).
* **Camera:** Built-in laptop webcam or any basic USB webcam.


### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

We will use a "Landmark-based" approach rather than raw image pixels to make it incredibly fast.

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **Hand Tracking Engine:** **Google MediaPipe**.
* *Why?* It provides extremely fast, pre-trained hand landmark detection that runs on any CPU (no GPU needed).


* **Classification Logic:** **Scikit-learn (Random Forest)** or a simple **K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)**.
* *Approach:* Instead of "looking" at the image, the AI looks at the *distance* between fingers.


* **UI/Frontend:** **Streamlit** (with the `streamlit-webrtc` component for real-time video).
* **Speech Output:** **gTTS (Google Text-to-Speech)** or **pyttsx3**.
* **Data Collection:** Students can create their own "Dataset" in 10 minutes by recording themselves making gestures and saving the hand coordinates to a CSV file.
