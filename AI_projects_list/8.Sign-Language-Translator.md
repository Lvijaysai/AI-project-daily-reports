## Project 8: Sign Language Translator

### Project Abstraction

The **Sign Language Translator** is a real-time communication bridge for the hearing and speech impaired. Using a standard webcam, the application detects hand landmarks and identifies the specific gestures corresponding to the Sign Language alphabet or common words (e.g., "Hello," "Thank You," "Help").

The AI analyzes the coordinates of 21 points on the human hand to recognize patterns. Once a gesture is identified, the app displays the corresponding text on the screen and uses **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** to announce it, enabling a natural flow of conversation.
