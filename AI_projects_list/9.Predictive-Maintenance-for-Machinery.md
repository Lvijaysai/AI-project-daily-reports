
## Project 9: Predictive Maintenance for Machinery

### Project Abstraction

The **Predictive Maintenance** system acts as a "health monitor" for industrial equipment like motors, pumps, or fans. Instead of waiting for a machine to break, this AI analyzes **Time-Series Data**—specifically vibration patterns and sound frequencies.

Machines in good condition have a consistent "signature." When a bearing starts to wear out or a belt loosens, the vibration frequency shifts. The model uses **Anomaly Detection** to identify these subtle changes. The professional output is a "Remaining Useful Life" (RUL) estimate, allowing factory managers to schedule repairs during non-working hours, saving thousands in lost production.

### Hardware Requirements

* **Development Phase:**
* **CPU:** Standard laptop (8GB RAM).
* **Sensors (Optional for testing):** Students can use a **Smartphone's built-in Accelerometer** (using apps like "Physics Toolbox") to collect real vibration data from a household appliance (like a washing machine or hair dryer).


* **Data Source:** If sensors aren't available, students should use the **NASA Randomized Milling Dataset** or the **AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset** (both free on Kaggle).

### Simple & 100% Free Tech Stack

We will treat this as a "Time-Series Classification" problem.

* **Language:** **Python 3.9+**
* **ML Library:** **Scikit-learn** (using **Isolation Forest** for anomaly detection) or **Prophet** (by Meta, for forecasting).
* **UI/Frontend:** **Streamlit**. (Excellent for showing live "Guage" charts and "Health Status: Green/Yellow/Red").
* **Data Visualization:** **Plotly**. (Crucial for showing the "Vibration Waveform" over time).
* **Signal Processing:** **SciPy**. (Used for "Fast Fourier Transform" or FFT—a professional way to turn raw sound/vibration into frequency data).
* **Deployment:** **Streamlit Community Cloud**.
